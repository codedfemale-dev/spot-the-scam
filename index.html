About the game & trusted guidance

Created by Coded Female to make cyber security more approachable. Phishing means using deceptive messages to trick someone into revealing information or taking an unsafe action. These scenarios are fictional. Example addresses are deliberately non-working text.

No answers or personal details are stored or sent anywhere. Progress resets when you reload. Fonts load from Google Fonts; the game works with fallback fonts too.

Further reading: NCSC: spotting scams, NCSC: reporting suspicious emails, and CISA: multi-factor authentication. For work accounts, follow your organisation’s reporting and security procedures.
